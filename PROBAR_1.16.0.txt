PRUEBA ARLIGHTBOSSES 1.16.0

Bloques:
- arlightbosses:copper_treasure_chest
- arlightbosses:iron_treasure_chest
- arlightbosses:pony_treasure_chest
- arlightbosses:diamond_treasure_chest
- arlightbosses:netherite_treasure_chest

Ejemplo:
/setblock ~ ~ ~ arlightbosses:copper_treasure_chest[facing=north,chest_state=closed]
/setblock ~ ~ ~ arlightbosses:copper_treasure_chest[facing=north,chest_state=opening]
/setblock ~ ~ ~ arlightbosses:copper_treasure_chest[facing=north,chest_state=opened]

Cada bloque debe mostrar su modelo, textura y animación correspondiente.
El inventario personal solo funciona cuando ArlightBingo lo registra dentro de una dungeon.
